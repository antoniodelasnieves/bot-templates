## 1. Where Opus is off

Too optimistic on conversion, too cheap on price, too ready to spend.

Import-to-lead at 2% (his base) is the sharp error. A working free template removes the reason to hire him. I use 0.5%. A 20% close on "DM me" is a trained sale; I use 10%. Click-to-import at 20% skips creating an account; I use 10%. With no baseline, 3,000 impressions is an upside used as the midpoint. I use 1,000. EUR 900 teaches a cheap price; I use EUR 1,800 and 50% contribution, not 60%. "Paid clicks convert at half" is unmeasured.

My funnel, 8 posts a month. Paid CAC keeps his half-rate rule and CPM of 8 (base) and 6 (high):

| | Low | Base | High |
|---|---|---|---|
| Impressions per post | 200 | 1,000 | 5,000 |
| Click rate | 0.3% | 0.8% | 1.5% |
| Click to import | 5% | 10% | 20% |
| Import to lead | 0.2% | 0.5% | 1.5% |
| Lead to paid | 5% | 10% | 20% |
| Clients a month | ~0 | 0.003 | 0.36 |
| Deal (EUR) | 1,200 | 1,800 | 2,500 |
| Paid CAC (EUR) | n/a | ~40,000 | ~1,300 |

His base is one setup every 5 months. Mine is about one every 26 years. His high case wins only if every rate is at its ceiling together. On his own base, three months of expected contribution is about EUR 310, against a EUR 450 test. That test is not "covered by one client."

Right: no affiliate; rewards are not a plan (press only; he is in Spain); English X will not fill a Spanish pipeline.
