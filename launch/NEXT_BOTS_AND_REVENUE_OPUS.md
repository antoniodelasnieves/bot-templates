## 3. Does it need heavy marketing? No

Assumptions: 8 posts a month; paid click rate is half of organic; contribution is 60% of the deal; capacity is 3 setups a month alongside his job.

| Step | Low | Base | High |
|---|---|---|---|
| Impressions per post | 800 | 3,000 | 8,000 |
| Click rate | 0.5% | 1% | 1.5% |
| Click to import | 10% | 20% | 30% |
| Import to lead | 0.5% | 2% | 2.5% |
| Lead to paid | 10% | 20% | 25% |
| Organic clients a month | 0.002 | 0.19 | 1.8 |
| Deal (EUR) | 500 | 900 | 1,500 |
| Contribution per client (EUR) | 300 | 540 | 900 |
| CPM (EUR) | 10 | 8 | 6 |
| Paid CAC (EUR) | ~80,000 | ~2,000 | ~430 |
| Max profitable paid spend a month (EUR) | 0 | 0 | ~510 |

CPM is the cost per 1,000 promoted impressions.

- **Break-even** is CAC at or below contribution. The base case misses by about 3.7x.
- **High-case cap:** 1.2 spare setups a month times EUR 430 is about EUR 510.
- **3-month test:** at most EUR 450 total, which one base-case client's contribution covers. Start only after one real organic enquiry. Stop after EUR 300 if nothing comes in.
- **The offer, not reach, is the constraint.**
