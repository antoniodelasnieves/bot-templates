# Launch plan v2

Serial plan for four free Public Grok Bot templates. Each step finishes, and its exit gate passes, before the next step starts. This file does not post anything and does not change a template.

The linkless thread from 12:03 Madrid on 29 September 2026 was deleted. Do not refer to it in public. Do not rebuild it. There is no live promise to repair.

Hard rules:

- Never post about a template until that template is published and its share link opens logged out.
- Nothing is posted until Antonio has approved the exact text, including the real URL in place of `[LINK]`.
- No invented metrics, user counts, hours saved, or discounts.
- The templates are free.
- Dates below are a suggestion. If a gate slips, every later step slips. Do not post early to protect a date.

Owners:

- Source bot: the bot that already holds that template. It drafts polish and later raises that template's publish card. It does not press confirm and it does not post.
- Antonio: accepts or rejects wording, presses confirm on each card himself, checks the link logged out, approves exact post text, and posts.
- Reviewer: a Grok 4.7 review against the rubric in step 1. It scores. It does not publish and it does not post.

Clock: Europe/Madrid. Until the last Sunday of October 2026, Madrid is on CEST (UTC+2), 6 hours ahead of US Eastern and 9 hours ahead of US Pacific. 17:30 Madrid = 11:30 US Eastern = 08:30 US Pacific. After that Sunday, recompute the US columns. Do not move the Madrid times.

D0 is the calendar day the first share link passes the private-window check. Posting does not start on D0 unless the Phase 2 gate has also passed (all four links work). If the gate passes before 16:00 Madrid, the first X post is that day at 17:30. If it passes after 16:00, the first X post is the next weekday at 17:30.

## Rubric used at every Phase 1 gate

Score 1 to 10. Pass is 9 or 10. The reviewer writes the score and one paragraph. A 9 requires all of these:

1. The public name matches the profile name. The card does not say "deal" and does not promise a discount.
2. The first sentence of the storefront states the job. A safety limit is on the card.
3. The first-run message fits on one phone screen and is the screenshot. It states what the bot will not do.
4. Getting-started asks one thing at a time. Any clock time shows the form HH:MM and one example.
5. The polish adds no power. The bot still does not send, spend, book, or message anyone else beyond what the recipe already allows.
6. Antonio can walk the questions with fake answers and reach the "create the routine?" yes, or the equivalent confirm already in the recipe.

A score under 9 returns to the same step once. A second score under 9 stops that template. Antonio decides whether to cut scope or drop it. Later templates wait. Nothing in Phase 2 starts.

## Phase 1. Polish

Exit for the phase: all four templates have a reviewer score of 9 or higher, and Antonio has accepted the wording. Budget about 2 hours of Antonio's time per template (source-bot draft, his edit, one review). A failed review adds about an hour. This is wording and question clarity, not new skills.

Suggested start: Wednesday 30 September 2026, one template in the morning and one in the afternoon. Phase 1 then ends Thursday 1 October if both days pass on the first review. One template per day instead ends Monday 5 October. Use the slower pace if a review fails.

### Step 1. Polish Promise Tracker: No Proof, Not Done

Owner: source bot drafts, Antonio accepts, reviewer scores.

Why first: the storefront line is already the post. The setup question that asks for timezone, workdays, and workday start and end in one breath is the conversion bug. The recipe says one question at a time.

Polish:

- Name stays `Promise Tracker: No Proof, Not Done`.
- Storefront, three short sentences: `"OK, on it" is not done. I keep each promise open until you confirm proof that matches the bar, and I draft the chase for you to send. I only message you, in this chat.`
- First-run message stays the screenshot. Keep the current hello. Add one line at the end so the next action is visible: `First question:` and then question 1 only.
- Split the current question 2 into three asks: timezone; which days are workdays (default Monday to Friday); workday start and workday end as HH:MM, with the example `09:00` and `18:00`.
- On the stale-hours and chase-time questions, keep the defaults (2 hours; 12:30 and 17:00) visible in the question.
- Do not put a real person's name, a real promise, or a pasted screenshot of proof into the opening. The screenshot for later is this hello only.

Effort: about 2 hours.

Exit gate: reviewer score 9 or higher on this template only. Then step 2.

### Step 2. Polish Daily Habit Check-in

Owner: source bot drafts, Antonio accepts, reviewer scores.

Polish:

- Name stays `Daily Habit Check-in`.
- Storefront, three sentences: `A one-minute daily check-in and a five-minute weekly review. Areas: health habits, career and learning, family and relationships, and money habits, each with a narrow limit. I only message you, and I do not give medical, mental-health, legal, tax, or financial advice.`
- First-run message: keep the hello and the professional redirect. Add `I only message you, in this chat.` The crisis rules stay in memory. They are not the screenshot.
- Split the current first question into two: timezone; then quiet hours as HH:MM-HH:MM, example `22:00-07:00`.
- Before goal-setting, one sentence: `One small goal per area is enough. A second only if you ask.` That matches the recipe (one per area, a second only if asked, max two).
- Say that no clock time ships with the template, so a skipped time is asked again rather than filled in.

Effort: about 2 hours.

Exit gate: reviewer score 9 or higher. Then step 3.

### Step 3. Polish Family Getaway Finder

Owner: source bot drafts, Antonio accepts, reviewer scores.

Polish:

- Name stays `Family Getaway Finder`. Do not rename it toward the branch slug. The recipe says it never promises discounts.
- Storefront: keep the shortlist, free cancellation, and "you book" lines. Add `It never promises a discount.` Keep `never books, reserves, holds or pays`.
- First-run message: keep `I never book or pay; you do.` Add `I store the party as counts of adults and children, not names.`
- Quiet-hours question uses the same form as the others: HH:MM-HH:MM, example `21:00-08:00`, not "21:00 to 08:00" as the only form.
- Screenshot is that hello, including the booking refusal. No hotel name, no price, no child's name. Those are not known at import, and the recipe forbids inventing prices.

Effort: about 2 hours.

Exit gate: reviewer score 9 or higher. Then step 4.

### Step 4. Polish Bot Team Health Check

Owner: source bot drafts, Antonio accepts, reviewer scores.

Polish:

- Name stays `Bot Team Health Check`.
- Storefront, three sentences: `Reviews how your Grok Bot assistants are set up and returns one numbered list of fixes. It does not edit, create, or remove another bot. Sending, spending, posting, and deleting need your yes.`
- Keep the "say get started" gate. Nothing runs before it, and that is the recipe. The conversion fix is to make the required words obvious, not to remove them.
- First-run message, screenshot length: `Hi, I'm your bot team health checker. I review how your assistants are set up. I never edit another bot, and I never send, post, email, spend, delete, or create or remove a bot without your yes. Say "get started". Then seven questions, one at a time. Timezone is the only one with no default.`
- Fix the nested quotes so "get started" is easy to read on a phone.
- Do not screenshot a real roster. The hello is the image.

Effort: about 2 hours.

Exit gate: reviewer score 9 or higher.

Phase 1 gate: steps 1 through 4 have each passed, and Antonio has the accepted wording for all four. Phase 2 does not start on a template that failed.

## Phase 3. X, then LinkedIn

One bot per slot. The slot is 17:30 Madrid. Skip Saturday and Sunday for a new bot. Each X slot is one template that already passed Phase 2. The import URL is in the post body, not only in a reply. One real screenshot from Phase 2 is attached. No hashtag stuffing, no request for likes, reposts, follows, or keyword replies.

Hashtag count, sourced: Sprout Social's current guidance, as reported by the U.S. Chamber of Commerce, is 1 to 2 hashtags on X and 3 to 5 on LinkedIn. https://www.uschamber.com/co/grow/marketing/how-to-use-hashtags

The specific tags below are (opinion). They are labels, not a claim that anyone is searching them today.

- X, one tag on every template post: `#Grok`
- LinkedIn bundle, four tags: `#Grok` `#ArtificialIntelligence` `#Productivity` `#FutureOfWork`

Do not add `#AI`, `#Travel`, `#Deals`, or `#Habits` on X. (opinion) A second broad tag does not help a post that already names the product, and the X cap in the source above is two.

Mentions: do not tag or @ mention `@grok` or `@xai` on these posts. (opinion) A mention notifies an account that did not ask for the launch. No public source used here says a mention improves distribution of a template. If Antonio later wants a product note to either account, that is a separate post he writes and approves. It is not part of these five posts.

X Article versus the post: the short post is what distributes. The X Article is the long read, with the same screenshot and the same URL in the last line. Secondary guide, not an official help page (help.x.com was not readable in the prior review): an Article can carry a title, headings, and images, and needs X Premium. https://tweetloft.com/blog/how-to-use-twitter-x-long-form-posts-notes

Prepare the article from the existing draft in `launch/STRATEGY.md` section 3, updated so it matches the polished wording. That prep is inside the slot, before the clock. If the article is not ready 15 minutes before 17:30, the short post still goes out. (opinion) A working link beats a perfect article. The article then has to be done, with the URL in it, before the next bot's slot. It does not get its own extra announcement.

Reply window: 17:30 to 18:45 Madrid on the new post. Next morning 08:15 to 08:30, a sweep of overnight replies. Answer a real question with the rule from that template. Correct a factual miss once. Do not run two live threads in one window.

Why the URL is in the body: the published For You defaults weight an opened link at 0.2 and do not show a negative "has a link" weight. Weights scale predicted probabilities, not raw counts. https://github.com/xai-org/x-algorithm/blob/a707cc27ba36d3fa79450c9cffcc48a82d080b02/home-mixer/params/param.rs and https://github.com/xai-org/x-algorithm/blob/a707cc27ba36d3fa79450c9cffcc48a82d080b02/README.md

The same README's `AgeFilter` drops For You candidates older than 48 hours. A Monday recap that only says "see last week's thread" points at posts that filter has removed. The recap repeats the four URLs in the post body.

Measurement, written down at 24 hours and again at 72 hours, per post: bookmarks and link clicks. Impressions are context. Likes are not the row to manage. No target number is set here. After the recap, rank the four URLs by link clicks. That rank chooses what to explain next. It does not change what the templates do.
